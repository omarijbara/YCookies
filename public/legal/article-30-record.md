# Record of Processing Activities 
*(Pursuant to GDPR Article 30)*

**Organization Name:** YCookies
**Role:** Data Processor (on behalf of customers) / Data Controller (for customer billing/account data)

## 1. Categories of Processing (As a Data Processor)

**Purpose of Processing:**
To provide a Cookie Consent Management Platform (CMP) and reverse-proxy script filtering service to hosted domains.

**Categories of Data Subjects:**
End-users (visitors) of websites utilizing the YCookies service.

**Categories of Personal Data:**
- **Hashed IP Addresses:** (One-way salted SHA-256 hash). We do not store raw IP addresses in long-term storage.
- **User Agent Strings:** Browser, OS, and device characteristics.
- **Consent Preferences:** Audit trails of which cookie categories (essential, marketing, analytics) were consented to or rejected and corresponding TCF strings.

**Categories of Recipients:**
Data is strictly accessible by the respective Data Controller (our customer) through the YCookies dashboard, and authorized YCookies system administrators for maintenance.

**Transfers to Third Countries:**
No data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA) without Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) in place. Infrastructure is primarily hosted via Hetzner (Germany).

**Retention Policy:**
Consent logs are retained based on the customer's configuration (default 365 days). After the retention period lapses, our scheduled deletion routines permanently purge the specific records.

## 2. Categories of Processing (As a Data Controller)

**Purpose of Processing:**
Account management, billing, and customer support.

**Categories of Data Subjects:**
Customers (Tenant Admins, Agency Managers) utilizing the YCookies service.

**Categories of Personal Data:**
- Name, Email Address, Billing information (processed via Stripe).
- Support communications and metadata.

**Categories of Recipients:**
Payment gateways (Stripe), email service providers, and internal administrative tooling.

**Retention Policy:**
Customer details are retained for the duration of the business contract and for standard accounting/tax periods afterward (typically 7 years).
